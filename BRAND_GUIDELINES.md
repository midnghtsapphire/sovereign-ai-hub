# Brand Guidelines — Sovereign AI Hub

## Brand pillars

- **Sovereignty:** the user owns the stack and the outcome
- **Privacy:** prompts/data stay local; minimize collection
- **Power:** GPU-accelerated, “serious tool” feel
- **Clarity:** fewer buzzwords, more concrete proof points

## Voice and tone

- Direct, confident, and technical without being alienating
- Avoid moralizing language; focus on capability and control
- Use short sentences and strong verbs

## Messaging primitives

- “Runs 100% local”
- “No accounts required”
- “Works offline”
- “Your AI. Your rules.”

## Visual direction

- Dark cinematic theme with neon accents
- High-contrast typography and spacious layout
- Use gradients sparingly as emphasis (hero headlines, section highlights)

## Typography

- Primary: Inter
- Monospace / code: JetBrains Mono

## Do / Don’t

- Do: use concrete capability bullets (offline, local, GPU)
- Do: emphasize user control and data ownership
- Don’t: claim “secure” without a specific mechanism
- Don’t: imply unlawful uses; keep examples neutral and professional
