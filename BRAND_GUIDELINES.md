# Brand Guidelines

## Brand Positioning

Sovereign AI Hub is confident, privacy-forward, and developer-friendly. The tone is direct: sovereignty, control, and local execution.

## Voice & Tone

- Clear, assertive, anti-buzzword.
- Technical credibility without jargon overload.
- User-first: emphasize control, privacy, and performance tradeoffs honestly.

## Visual System (Current Site)

- Theme: dark, cinematic, neon-accent.
- Primary accent: teal/green glow (mapped to `--primary`).
- Secondary accent: warm gold (mapped to `--accent`).

## Typography

- Sans: Inter
- Mono: JetBrains Mono

## UI Principles

- High contrast, readable typography.
- Strong CTA hierarchy (primary vs outline).
- Motion as accent (subtle glow/pulse), not distraction.

## Asset Notes

If a logo/mark is introduced later, add:

- Source files (SVG) in `public/`
- Minimum clear-space rules
- Light/dark variants
